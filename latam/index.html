Christmas 2026 in Peru, Colombia, Ecuador

Desk research on holiday traditions, insights, social context and activations. Four reports in English, sources linked under every fact.

🕛

Christmas traditions in Peru, Colombia and Ecuador

How each market spends December: which night matters most, the days between 24 and 27 December, the local layers on the Christian calendar, and lottery and Reyes equivalents.

🛵

Holiday insights: delivery, miracles, relationships

New Year attitudes, what couriers, customers and small businesses live through in December, which miracles people believe in, and how relationships behave around the holiday.

🧭

Social context 2026 and small-business life

The mood of each market going into Christmas 2026 (politics, money, security, migration) and what small businesses deal with all year.

🎁

Christmas and New Year activations review

Global and local Christmas and New Year activations reconstructed mechanic by mechanic: what was built, what people did and how it spread.

 🧭

Small-business owners: who they are and how they live

A socio-demographic and lifestyle portrait of small-business owners in Peru, Colombia and Ecuador: who they are, how the business runs, how they are paid and borrow, and where the data stops.
